The preceding “mature crew” pair was correctly rejected as childish: longer legs alone did not remove thick rounded contours, featureless beige masks, mitten hands and flared toy feet. This redraw addresses those causes rather than only changing height.

- **Faces:** both now preserve angular three-quarter facial planes, projecting nose geometry, oblique jaw lines and narrow slanted eyes. Neither has the .48 attempt's smiling/dot-eye expression. Mara's face is a narrow partial profile; Neri's broad ochre side plane makes the cheek/nose structure stronger. These are severe graphic faces rather than round doll masks. The line eyes imply a reserved/squinting expression and can read as closed eyes; that is a stylistic limit, not a claim of realistic expression.
- **Bodies and stance:** heads are compact relative to lean torsos and long limbs. Visible necks, small wrists and hands, narrow ankles and distinct restrained boots improve adult anatomy. Both have a weight-bearing leg and a bent counterbalancing leg. The stances remain designed and slightly stylized, but the previous oversized hands and toy-like feet are absent.
- **Matching language:** contour weight, hard corners, flat color density, facial plane treatment and garment scale agree. Mara's short rust mantle and broad shield distinguish her from Neri's long teal coat and ivory hood without changing rendering style. Both use the shared five-color family. Neither has boot shine, paint texture, hair strands, drawstrings or microdecorations.
- **Remaining limitations:** Mara's waist has a small ivory triangular notch and her facial nose line is slightly more irregular than Neri's. Her ochre helmet is still abstract and cap-like, although the straight faceted outline and side guard no longer read as the previous baseball cap/hair tuft. Neri's face is more severe and slightly larger within its hood. These differences do not recreate the prior age/detail mismatch, and a global regeneration risks returning the rejected smile/roundness.
