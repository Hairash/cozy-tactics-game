# Mature crew pair — Astra critical review

Reviewed raw/ally-1.png and raw/ally-2.png after the second shared ComfyUI pass (denoise .44, style .20, composition .95, CFG 2.5, 8 steps, seed 428613). This assessment covers the generated source pair; final 80px alpha/export inspection belongs to the main pass.

**Decision: suitable for the next human pilot review. No material blocker requiring another generation. This is not production approval.**

- **Consistency:** both use the same near-black blue-green contours, flat ivory face/front panel, dark solid trousers, ochre equipment and restrained rust/teal outer garment. Contour thickness, face size, head-to-body ratio and information density now agree. Neither has a different painting or lighting treatment.
- **Maturity:** long bent legs, compact heads, angular hood/helmet silhouette and unsmiling faces remove the strongest child/chibi cues of the rejected pair. Neri is narrower and more reserved; Mara is broader through the shield. Both remain deliberately stylized rather than anatomically realistic.
- **Shape interest:** bent knees and off-centre torsos provide readable asymmetry. Neri's two elbows and the open triangular gap below the staff arm are particularly clear. Mara's large shield gives a separate silhouette; her inward forearm remains legible. These are distinct roles with one shared shape language.
- **Eyes:** Neri has the requested short horizontal lines. Mara's eyes are broader flattened wedges/ovals rather than equally thin strokes, but clearly horizontal and no longer dots or vertical cartoon eyes. This is the main residual difference. At small size they may read as a heavier stern gaze; do not claim the eye shapes match exactly.
- **Unwanted detail:** no boot highlights, hair strands, buckles, embroidery, face highlights, ground shadows or garment shading. Neri's unwanted hoodie strings disappeared. Mara's cap no longer has the baseball visor; its rounded ochre crown reads as a simple helmet/cap and remains less angular than the guide.
- **Residual softness:** Mara's exposed fist is rounded and a few outer corners are softened by generation. Neri's head opening and feet are more angular. Neither difference creates the previous mismatch in style or maturity, so another global generation risks losing the successful restraint for little benefit.

For the final 80px export, preserve the thin staff and elbow gaps, use equal approximately 62px figure heights including staff, avoid outlines added during post-processing, and inspect faces on the exact preview background. Show both together with the rejected raster80 version; the user should judge the mature-versus-childish boundary directly.

The 512px guide files are composition conditioning only. This review concerns ComfyUI-generated raster output. Final runtime assets must remain at most 80×80.
