# Independent assessment — processed painterly pilot

Reviewed `reviews/pilot-assets.png` directly. **Presentable style exploration after one bounded identity/alpha repair round; not an accepted production set.** Single-subject output is substantially more consistent than v1. Cream/teal/brass palette and ink treatment form a plausible world, but role readability and common shape language still need work. Contact sheet alone cannot verify actual board footprint, river connectivity, anchor consistency or alpha edges at native resolution.

## Targeted repair priorities

1. **Tree — blocking defect.** Only trunk/root vegetation remains in the processed sheet, with an enormous empty canopy region. If raw canopy exists, repair extraction rather than regenerate the subject. Preserve pale leaf interiors while removing only background-connected pixels; check fine foliage on dark/light swatches. Remove the baked grassy ground patch and shadow if separable. If no usable canopy exists, one replacement with three broad angular foliage masses is warranted.
2. **Mara — role identity.** Current subject carries a pointed dagger and no broad ram guard; reads as a light rogue. Preserve her practical hood, work-cloak palette and human warmth. Replace the dagger with an empty right hand and add a large trapezoid cream/brass left forearm guard, broad enough to read at 48–64 px. Broaden stance and shorten proportions modestly. No need to redesign face or costume details.
3. **Neri — role identity and cohesion.** Current sprite has oversized glossy eyes, a round youthful face and curled bell-tipped hat, distinctly more storybook/chibi than Mara. More urgently, no tide staff or guiding hand distinguishes her ability. Add one clearly separated brass hooked tide-gauge staff, keep its tip inside canvas and its shaft thicker than extraction noise, and expose a simple guiding hand. Reduce eye shine/roundness and replace bell flourish with an angular hood fold if a bounded revision permits. Do not lose her readable light coat/dark hood contrast.
4. **Breaker — silhouette identity.** Current robot has slender limbs, small claw hands and a broad empty leg gap; reads as a friendly general service robot. Widen dark torso and shoulders, shorten legs, replace claws with oversized blunt rectangular ram forearms. Keep cream ceramic and amber sensor but simplify little joints. It must read heavier than Sentinel at equal ground scale.

## Acceptance boundaries

The set is not yet coherent in proportions: Mara is relatively tall, Neri compact and round, Sentinel needlelike, Breaker tall and thin. Match relative ground anchors/scales in the scene before judging whether revisions fixed this. Sprite cards each fitting their own rectangle can conceal those differences.

After the bounded repair round, show the user both the complete contact sheet and a square-grid gameplay-scale scene with labels/health/flow/fan direction rendered at runtime. Include raw-versus-processed tree comparison and disclose remaining emitter, wall-footprint and material-texture caveats. Inspect all extracted sprites against cream/teal/charcoal backgrounds. Require user pilot approval before any catalog production, production replacement or publishing. No mechanics changes are necessary to address these defects.
