## Remaining caveats to disclose

- **Cohesion is partial.** Regenerated allies use flat large graphic wedges and compact faces; the tree, column, wall and fan retain more painted texture, small linework and volume. They share muted cream/teal/brass colors, but do not yet look fully drawn by one hand. The tree is the most detailed organic object; Breaker retains smooth torso gradients and shiny eye dots. Let the user choose whether to simplify props or add restrained painterly texture to characters before scaling the catalog.
- **Mara communicates defensive gear, with a fidelity compromise.** Wooden handheld shield with a teal diamond replaces the requested cream/brass forearm ram guard. Her frontal face and compact cloak are much better; the shield reads at small scale. It can suggest ramming, but the equipment language is more medieval than harbor infrastructure. Do not claim exact specification compliance.
- **Neri reads as a water caster, with a fidelity compromise.** Trident staff is legible and silhouette now distinct from Mara. It is not the specified hooked tide gauge, and turquoise eye mask/gloves shift her toward a magical heroine. Her long separated staff remains an extraction/phone-size edge risk; visible thinness should be checked on final alpha swatches.
- **Breaker now passes broad identity.** Blocky torso, blunt forearms and short heavy feet clearly distinguish it from the thin Sentinel. Original raw image includes a ground shadow; final processing should avoid a second competing shadow. Twin shiny eyes are friendlier and more toy-like than the small sensor target, but no longer an organic monster.
- **Tree repair succeeds at canopy retention, not full style matching.** Many thin twigs, leaf gaps and orange fruit remain richer than the three-mass target. Fine light speckling at some contour edges may be matting halo; inspect on cream, dark teal and charcoal before production. Root vegetation still partly acts as a baked ground patch.
- **Scene-scale role clarity has limits.** At 64 px tiles the broad machine, pointed sentry and cloaked allies remain distinguishable. At 40 px tiles faces, shield detail and staff head become tiny; Mara/Neri depend mainly on staff versus shield silhouette. The Sentinel's needle legs and tree twigs are fragile. No need to add detail to solve this.
- **Props retain earlier caveats.** Wall still resembles two steps/loose blocks rather than one closed obstacle; fan's thin grill is dense and its pedestal feels domestic; column is more slender/classical than the angular target. Single-cell base placement is plausible in the scene, but artwork alone does not explain blocking/toppling/fan direction.
- **Terrain is a composition study.** Continuous banks join visibly in these screenshots, but textured water is still somewhat plasterlike. Ground/grass are more granular than the graphic characters. Screenshots do not demonstrate every river socket, source/end, bridge, freeze variant, board mask edge or dynamic direction change.
- **Gameplay overlays remain unproven.** Screenshots show team ground rings and subtle grid; they do not show health/status stacks, current arrows, rotated fan arrows, selection and neighboring occlusion under actual play. The scene is explicitly static. Do not present it as a verified runtime replacement or completed tactical readability test.
