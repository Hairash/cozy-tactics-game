# Painted return — final-size critical review

Reviewed current reviews/raster80-assets.png at its80/64/40px comparisons against archive/painterly-pilot/contact-sheet.png. Also inspected final80px wall, fan and Neri PNGs directly. The inspected sheet precedes the main pass's planned additional column widening.

**Decision: faithful restoration of the first painted direction, ready for human review. No material stylistic mismatch calls for another redesign.**

The fine dark drawing, broad muted painted surfaces and small handmade irregularities now match the first pilot. The result retains paint and volume rather than the rejected flat vector geometry. Crew, robots and props share teal/ivory/ochre with restrained contrast. Mara's rust garment and shield clearly separate her from ivory/teal Neri and the hook staff. Neri's corrected warm face no longer reads as a mismatched teal mask. At80px the difference in source facial detail is minor; at40px silhouette and equipment carry identification.

Breaker and fan preserve the original material treatment; the fan has not become a photographic object. The fan's transparent blade gaps and exposed pedestal silhouette read correctly in the inspected PNG. The tree remains the densest asset, with finer branching than the figures, but that is inherited from the requested first pilot and remains compatible at80px. At40px its foliage compresses into a softer cluster; avoid reintroducing an abstract geometric tree to solve this.

Footprint caveats: the column is visually narrow in this sheet, so the planned width adjustment is justified. Sentinel intentionally has little filled area because of its tripod legs; forcing equal opaque area would destroy the original design. The low two-stone wall should remain short and wide. Its dark right edge reads as the stone's side at final size; technical alpha checks should distinguish that physical edge from detached cast-shadow pixels. This visual review alone does not prove every alpha pixel is correct.

Terrain remains quiet relative to sprites and follows the original painted palette. Remaining detail differences are moderate differences between materials, not competing art styles. Final acceptance belongs to the user; no production or mass-catalog approval is implied.
