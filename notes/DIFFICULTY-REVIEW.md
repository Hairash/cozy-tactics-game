# Campaign difficulty review

Every old and new route replayed through legal public gameplay inputs, recording actual victory, damage, losses and actions. Difficulty labels are design estimates from AI stage, survival margin and encounter demands; low-budget beam search is a sensitivity check, not a human-skill model. Browser playtests cover animation, interaction and selected battles, not 100 manual UI playthroughs.

All 100 revised missions have a legal winning playthrough. The opening ten preserve the original AI. Later stages add hazard-aware positioning, target priorities, support coordination, guarded local relays and passenger extraction. No player statistics increase.
